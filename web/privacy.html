Examcamp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy covers the Examcamp website and the Examcamp Android app (package com.examcamp.app).

What we collect

  • Google account details (name, email address, profile photo) when you choose "Sign in with Google". Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication.
  • Your exam data: exam history and results, practice progress, and your app settings (language, theme, text size, AI settings). This is stored on your device and, when you are signed in, in our Supabase database so it follows you across devices.
  • Live exam data: if you join a live exam, your name, email, answers, score and submission time are stored so results and merit lists can be shown.
  • Your Gemini API key, if you enter one in Settings. It is stored on your device and synced with your account settings so you do not have to retype it. It is only used to send requests to Google's Gemini API on your behalf.

What we send to third parties

  • Google Gemini API: when you generate an AI exam, the topic text, instructions and any image or PDF you attach are sent to Google using your API key. Google handles that data under its own terms.
  • Google / Firebase (sign-in) and Supabase (database and question banks) process data on our behalf.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or advertising trackers.

Camera and files

The camera and file picker are used only when you tap "Capture" or "Attach" in AI Mode. The picture or file is used for that one question-generation request and is not uploaded to our servers.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. You can delete your account yourself: open the account menu (your picture, top right) and choose Delete account, in the website and in the app. This permanently removes your cloud data (account record, synced history and settings, practice progress, enrollment requests) and your sign-in. Live exam scores stay in the merit list without your name or e-mail. If you cannot sign in, email us from your sign-in address and we will remove it.

Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Access to cloud data is restricted by your signed-in account.

Children

Examcamp is intended for students preparing for exams. It is not directed at children under 13.
